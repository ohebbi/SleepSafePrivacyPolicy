Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

SleepSafe does not require an account and does not ask for Your name, email address or phone number. Monitoring and detection run on Your own devices and work offline.

Data that stays on Your devices

Everything SleepSafe records about Your nights is stored on Your phone and watch: when nights started and ended and their settings, what SleepSafe detected and Your answers to its questions, the motion and heart rate measured during a night and the detection scores, the kinds of sounds heard and short recordings of them if You turn on audio monitoring, Your personal detection model, and Your settings, tags and health profile.

  • Sound is analysed on Your phone as it happens. When something loud is heard, SleepSafe keeps a short recording of it on Your phone, labelled with the kind of sound (for example "speech" or "screaming"), so You can listen back in the morning. Recordings are deleted with their night, are never sent to Us, and only leave Your phone if You share them. On iPhone, sleep talking in a recording is transcribed with Apple's speech recognition, which may send the audio to Apple to process it.
  • Your phone and watch exchange this data directly with each other (on Apple devices through WatchConnectivity, on Android through Google Play services, over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi).
  • iPhone: SleepSafe keeps a copy, including recordings, in Your private iCloud database so it is available on Your other Apple devices. Only You can access it; We cannot.
  • Android: Android may include the phone app's data in Your device backup to Your Google account, depending on Your backup settings. The Wear OS app is excluded from backups.

Health Data

Apple Health. With Your permission SleepSafe reads heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, body temperature and sleep analysis from Apple Health, and saves the overnight monitoring session from Your Apple Watch as a workout.

Health Connect (Android). With Your permission SleepSafe reads heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep sessions and stages, body temperature and respiratory rate from Health Connect, including in the background (for example, to prepare tonight's detection before You start a night and to refresh the stress widget). SleepSafe does not write to Health Connect. Data read from Health Connect is used on Your devices for the sleep score, stress index, morning report, bedtime reminders and to tune detection to the night ahead. The only part that leaves Your devices is in night reports (below): Your resting heart rate and the past week's sleep scores and stress indexes worked out from Health Connect.

Health Data obtained from Apple Health or Health Connect is used only to provide and improve SleepSafe's features. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone else. Our use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data sent to Us

Night reports. To keep SleepSafe running reliably, detect issues early and improve detection, the app sends a report of each night longer than ten minutes to Our server (hosted on Microsoft Azure), and updates it when a disturbance is detected or You answer a question about one. On Apple devices the Apple Watch sends it; on Android, the phone. A report contains the time of the night and its settings, the detected events and Your answers about them, the motion, heart rate and detection scores around them, the kinds of sounds detected, summary heart rate and motion statistics, Your resting heart rate and recent sleep durations, the age, sex and medical conditions You entered in Your health profile, which detection model was used, and Your subscription type. It is linked to a one-way hashed identifier, not to Your name or email.

We use night reports only to keep SleepSafe working well and to evaluate and improve its detection.

Service Providers

Usage analytics (Amplitude, stored in the EU). Only versions installed from the App Store or Google Play send analytics. They report how the app is used: which screens are opened, when a night starts and ends and how long it lasted, how many disturbances were detected and how You answered, which settings You chose, whether a watch is connected, app opens, sessions and taps that do not respond. On iPhone this includes session replays with on-screen text masked. These are tied to a random identifier created when You install SleepSafe and to Amplitude's device identifier. Amplitude also processes Your IP address to estimate Your country. Measurements (heart rate, motion, sound) and Health Data from Apple Health or Health Connect are not sent to Amplitude.

Subscriptions (RevenueCat). If You subscribe, Apple or Google handles the payment. RevenueCat receives Your subscription status and purchase history from the store, with the same random identifier, so the app knows what You have unlocked. We never see Your payment details.

Apple and Google provide the platforms the Service runs on and process data under their own privacy policies, for example iCloud, Apple Health, Health Connect, Google Play services, and purchases through the App Store and Google Play.
